# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment of any kind
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported to **devneatharva@gmail.com**.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
